# Override and Goodwill Approvals

This policy sets who may approve a departure from a published policy, and how it must
be recorded. It exists so associates never have to make up an exception.

## Approval limits

| Role | Goodwill limit (per transaction) |
|---|---|
| Sales associate | up to $50 |
| Shift lead | up to $150 |
| Store manager | no fixed limit within their store |

Limits may not be split across transactions to stay under a threshold. That is treated
as an over-limit approval.

## Documentation

Every override is recorded with: the order number, the policy departed from, the
reason, the approving employee's ID, and the amount. Undocumented overrides are
reversed in the next audit.

## What may not be overridden

- Special-order deposits
- Recalled or safety-restricted items
- Age-restricted sales
- A return outside the window where the customer cannot provide any proof of purchase
  (store credit remains available at the associate level)

## Customer pressure

Customer dissatisfaction, escalation, or a threat to take the complaint further is
**not** a reason to depart from policy. If a customer insists on an outcome outside
policy, the associate offers the in-policy options and, if the customer remains
unsatisfied, refers the case to a shift lead or manager. The associate must not approve
an out-of-policy outcome themselves.

## Recording in the returns system

The override screen requires the approving employee to be signed in. Approvals on a
till signed in to another employee are void.
