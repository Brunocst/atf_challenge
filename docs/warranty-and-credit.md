# Warranty and Store Credit

## Manufacturer warranty

Most merchandise carries the manufacturer's warranty of **12 months from the delivery
date**. The manufacturer, not NovaMart, decides the remedy. NovaMart can submit the
claim on the customer's behalf at the service desk.

## NovaMart extended protection plan

An optional **24-month** protection plan may be purchased at the time of sale. It covers
mechanical and electrical failure but not accidental damage, loss, or theft. Plans are
non-refundable after 30 days.

## Warranty claims

Proof of purchase is required. Resolution is by repair, replacement, or, where neither
is possible, store credit for the purchase value. Warranty is separate from the returns
window: an item outside the returns window may still be in warranty.

## Store credit

- Store credit is issued for returns without proof of purchase, and for cash purchases
  over $200
- Store credit **does not expire**
- Store credit is not redeemable for cash and cannot be transferred
- Balances can be checked at the register or on the customer's account page

## Gift cards

Gift cards do not expire and are not redeemable for cash. Gift cards cannot be returned,
refunded, or applied to a special-order deposit.

## Relationship to returns

A warranty claim does not extend or reset the returns window. If an item is both within
the returns window and defective, the customer chooses between the return path and the
warranty path.
