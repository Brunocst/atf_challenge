# Standard Returns Policy (superseded)

> This document is no longer in force. It is retained for historical reference only.

## Eligibility window

Most merchandise may be returned within **60 calendar days of purchase**.

## Proof of purchase

A receipt is required. Returns without a receipt are accepted for store credit up to
$100.

## Condition requirements

Items must be in resalable condition with original packaging.

## Exclusions

Clearance merchandise, gift cards, and special orders are excluded.
