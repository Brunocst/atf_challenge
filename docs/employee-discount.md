# Employee Discount

## Value

Active NovaMart employees receive **20% off regular-price merchandise** in stores and
online.

## Eligibility

The discount applies to the employee's own purchases. It may be extended to a spouse or
domestic partner and dependent children living at the same address. Friends, extended
family, and customers may not use an employee discount, and using it for resale is
grounds for termination.

## Exclusions

- Gift cards and prepaid cards
- Clearance and final-sale merchandise
- Special orders and custom orders
- Protection plans and extended warranties
- Shipping and delivery charges
- Fuel, food and beverage, and pharmacy items

## Combination rules

The employee discount cannot be combined with a price match, another promotional
discount, or a coupon on the same item. Where an item qualifies for both, the employee
receives the larger single discount.

## Limits

Verification is by employee ID or badge at the register. The discount is capped at
**$1,500 of merchandise per calendar month** at the discounted value. Purchases above
the cap require HR approval.

## Termination

The discount ends on the employee's last day of employment. Purchases made after that
date are reversed.
