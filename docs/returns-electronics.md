# Opened Electronics Returns

This policy supplements the *Standard Returns Policy* for consumer electronics:
televisions, computers, tablets, phones, audio equipment, wearables, and gaming
hardware.

## Opened items

Electronics that have been opened or used may be returned within **14 calendar days
of the delivery date**. A **15% restocking fee** applies and is deducted from the
refund. All original accessories, cables, manuals, and the original packaging must be
included. Items missing components are refused.

## Unopened items

Electronics returned unopened and sealed are treated as standard merchandise and fall
under the **30-day** window in the *Standard Returns Policy*. No restocking fee applies.

## Not returnable once opened

The following cannot be returned after opening, regardless of the window:

- Software, digital codes, and game media with a broken seal
- Drones that have been flown
- Personal care devices (trimmers, hair tools, water flossers) once used
- In-ear headphones and earbuds, for hygiene reasons

## Defective electronics

An item that is defective out of the box is not subject to the restocking fee. Report
the defect within 14 days of delivery; the customer may choose a replacement or a full
refund. After 14 days, the manufacturer warranty applies (see *Warranty and Store
Credit*).
