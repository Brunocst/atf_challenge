# Standard Returns Policy

Applies to merchandise purchased in NovaMart stores or on novamart.example.com, for
customers in the United States.

## Eligibility window

Most merchandise may be returned within **30 calendar days of the delivery date**
(store purchases) or the date of delivery confirmation (online orders). Day 1 is the
day after delivery.

## Condition requirements

Items must be returned in resalable condition: original packaging where practical,
all accessories and manuals included, and no signs of use. Items that fail a condition
check may be refused or offered store credit at the manager's discretion.

## Proof of purchase

A receipt, order number, or a lookup on the customer's account is required. Without
any proof of purchase, the item may be accepted for **store credit only** and the
customer must present valid photo identification.

## Refund method

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within the 30-day window. Cash
purchases over $200 are refunded to store credit.

## Exclusions

Final-sale and clearance-marked merchandise, gift cards, protection plans, special
orders, and opened electronics are not covered by this policy. See the specific
policy for each: *Special Orders*, *Opened Electronics Returns*.

## Superseded versions

This policy replaced the previous 60-day window effective 2026-01-15. Any older
version of the returns policy is void.
