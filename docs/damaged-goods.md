# Damaged and Defective Goods on Arrival

## Reporting window

Damage that is visible on arrival must be reported within **48 hours of delivery**.
Report through the associate's returns screen or the customer's account page.

## Evidence required

- Photographs of the item and the packaging
- The order number
- A one-line description of the damage

## Resolution

Damaged-on-arrival items are resolved without a restocking fee and outside the normal
returns window:

- **Replacement** — same item shipped at no cost, where stock allows
- **Full refund** — including any shipping charges paid

The customer does not need to return the damaged item for low-value items under $50;
for higher-value items a prepaid return label is issued.

## Damage reported after 48 hours

Damage reported after 48 hours is handled under the *Standard Returns Policy*, with the
condition noted on the return record. The associate should record the damage even when
the return itself is outside the window.

## Damage in transit

For orders still in transit, or where the outer carton is damaged in front of the
customer, NovaMart files the carrier claim. The associate records the carrier name and
tracking number; the customer is never asked to contact the carrier.

## Relationship to other policies

This policy takes precedence over the windows in the *Standard Returns Policy* and
*Opened Electronics Returns* when the damage is reported within 48 hours.
