# Special and Custom Orders

A special order is any item the customer asks NovaMart to order in, configured to their
specification, or produced to order.

## Deposit

A **50% deposit** is collected when the order is placed. The deposit is applied to the
balance at pickup. The deposit is **non-refundable** if the customer cancels after
production begins.

## Lead times

Typical lead times are **4 to 8 weeks**. Lead times are estimates from the supplier and
are not guaranteed delivery dates. The customer is informed of the estimate at the time
of order and again if the supplier revises it.

## Cancellation

- Before production begins: full deposit refund, subject to any supplier restocking charge.
- After production begins: the deposit is forfeited and the order cannot be cancelled.
- After arrival, uncollected orders are held for 30 days, then closed and the deposit
  forfeited.

## Returns

Special orders are **not returnable** under the *Standard Returns Policy*. The only
exceptions are:

- Manufacturing defects, reported within **14 days of pickup**
- Items delivered in the wrong specification, where the error is NovaMart's

Both exceptions are resolved by replacement or full refund. Change-of-mind returns are
not accepted.

## Pricing

The price at the time of order is honoured. Later supplier or catalogue price changes
do not apply to an existing special order. Price matching does not apply to special
orders.
