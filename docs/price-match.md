# Price Match Policy

## Window

A price match may be requested within **14 calendar days of the purchase date**, or
within 14 days of delivery for online orders. After 14 days no price match is
available; the item may instead be returned under the *Standard Returns Policy* if it
is still within its window.

## Eligibility

The comparison item must be:

- Identical in brand, model, size, and colour
- In stock at an eligible competitor at the time of the request
- Advertised at a lower price in a publicly visible listing

## Eligible competitors

Price matches are accepted against these retailers only:

Amazon, Best Buy, Target, Walmart, Costco.

Any other retailer requires store manager approval.

## Exclusions

- Marketplace and third-party seller listings
- Membership-only, loyalty, or employee pricing
- Clearance, liquidation, open-box, or refurbished listings
- Bundle prices, gift-with-purchase, and rebate-after-purchase offers
- Prices from sites that ship to the US from outside the US

## How the match is paid

The difference is refunded to the original payment method. If the request comes within
14 days of purchase, no exchange is required — the customer keeps the item.

## Combination rules

A price match cannot be combined with the employee discount, a promotional discount, or
store credit redemption on the same item.
